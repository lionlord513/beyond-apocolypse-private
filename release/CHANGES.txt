BEYOND APOCALYPSE 1.0.10

- Added a player model selector in F9 with Default and SciFi Soldier appearances.

BEYOND APOCALYPSE 1.0.9

- Corrected native AK iron-sight alignment using its authored sight socket. Other rifles, hip-fire positioning and optic offsets retain their existing setup.
- Removed Performance Mode's repeated per-component scenery pass and forced visibility overrides. Native distance culling now remains under the game's control.
- Performance Mode retains native LOD, grass and post-processing reductions, skips unchanged settings, and restores previous values when disabled. Gains depend on existing quality settings and the scene; no minimum FPS is promised.
- Retained the 1.0.8 nitrous, material, motorcycle and inventory fixes. Personal developer tools remain excluded.

BEYOND APOCALYPSE 1.0.8
2026-11-02

- Corrected indirect lighting on nitrous bottles and custom rifles so their textures remain readable in shade. Normal, roughness and metallic maps now load in the correct colour space.
- Stage II nitrous emits purple exhaust flames; Stage I retains blue flames. Colour changes follow the active bottle stage.
- The native nitrous inspection percentage, fluid name and fill bar use the bottle stage colour: blue or purple.
- Replaced the large donor-barrel physics mesh with a compact bottle-sized collider. Pickup/drop restores the matching bottle scale.
- Nitrous bottles now mount horizontally with their placement origin between the two bracket feet. The valve and fluid-transfer interaction follow the new model orientation.
- Existing boost power, consumption, saved bottle identities, motorcycle setup and personal-menu separation are retained.

BEYOND APOCALYPSE 1.0.7
2026-11-02

- Added six refillable nitrous bottles: blue Stage I and purple Stage II, each in 2 L, 5 L and 10 L sizes.
- Nitrous is a separate native fluid with normal pouring and transfer controls. Attach bottles to a vehicle using the normal attachment controls, then hold Shift while driving to boost.
- Boost strength tapers at high speed and sharp steering, stops under braking or without engine power, and consumes nitrous. Multiple bottles extend supply without stacking force.
- Blue exhaust effects accompany boost. Bottles appear as uncommon building loot and can be purchased through Hangar.
- Added a compact digital vehicle dashboard with speed, gear, RPM, engine temperature, fluid levels, battery warnings and nitrous supply.
- The Hornet has a dedicated flight dashboard with collective, altitude and vertical speed; its existing Shift/Ctrl flight controls are retained.
- Retained 1.0.6 motorcycle seating, modular headlights, performance controls, weapons, backpacks and other existing fixes. Personal developer tools are excluded.

BEYOND APOCALYPSE 1.0.6

- Updated for Drive Beyond Horizons build 26106988 while retaining established vehicle, inventory, input and Hangar fixes.
- F9 opens player settings, statistics and host zombie population controls (2 to 40). Default targets are 5 by day and 10 at night or around an infested building.
- Bounded scenery updates replace repeated world-wide mesh scans; idle input and trailer searches are reduced.
- Both motorcycles retain corrected rider positioning, stable parked wheels and contact-height presentation. Removable bike-shaped headlights use the native car lighting system.
- Hornet pitch, roll and yaw remain enabled after resting on the ground; the manual collective gauge updates smoothly between flight calculations.
- Restored rotor-blade pickup recovery, replicated service-part fitting, Hangar click safeguards and saved control reconciliation.
- Public gameplay files only. Mod Manager is optional; the game can be launched directly through Steam.
