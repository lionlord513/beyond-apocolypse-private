# Hornet and landing-pad trailer

The Hornet needs an engine and both rotor blades to fly. Buy parts and a landing-pad trailer from the Hangar. New wild Hornets have two matching blades nearby.

## Controls

- Hover low over a parked landing-pad trailer and press **J** from the pilot seat. The Hornet docks, stops its engine and stows its blades.
- Press **J** again from the pilot seat, or while looking at the aircraft with empty hands, to release it. The engine stays off and the blades remain in the rack.
- Land and stop the rotor before removing a blade. Look at a fitted or racked blade and right-click **Remove**.
- Hold a blade, approach the matching side of the rotor mast and left-click **Attach** at the highlighted mount. Repeat on the other side. Blades can be fitted while docked.
- Hold a blade and look at the trailer's rack to store it. Each blade takes one backpack cell.
- Review or change gyrocopter controls in **F9 > Controls**.

Dock slowly with the trailer parked. People, cargo, occupied racks or another aircraft can block docking or release. Clear the pad and departure path before trying again.

## Saves and multiplayer

Everyone in co-op needs the same mod version. Close the game before updating.
Keep the mod's **state** folder with your game saves when moving a host world to another PC. Do not delete it to fix a docking problem.

See TEST-RESULTS.txt for the recorded tests and remaining multiplayer checks. Historical transport fixes remain in CHANGES.txt and the source's docs folder.
