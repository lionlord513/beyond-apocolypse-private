# Backpacks

Small bags hold 6 items, medium bags 12 and large bags 18. The gold cells are your hotbar slots. Each item takes one cell. Bags cannot hold other bags, attached car parts or fixed scenery.

## Controls

- **Tab:** open your worn bag or a ground bag within 2 metres. Tab or Esc closes it.
- With a bag worn and empty hands, look at a loose item within 2 metres and **right-click** to store it. Storage cells fill before free hotbar cells.
- **Drag Hands to an empty cell** to store the held item. Drag a cell to Hands to retrieve it. Drag between cells to move or swap items; release outside a cell to cancel.
- When holding a bag, **left-click** to equip or **right-click** to drop it with its contents.
- **B:** remove your worn bag to empty hands while on foot. In a vehicle, B remains autopilot.
- **Caps Lock:** toggle melee. Change melee bindings in **F9 > Controls**.
- Drag the title bar to move the grid. Hover over a cell to see the full item name.

Store a held hotbar weapon before moving its slot. You cannot open another player's held or worn bag. The game keeps running while the grid is open.

## Updating and removal

Everyone in co-op needs the same version. Close the game before updating or disabling the mod.
Before removing the mod, retrieve items from the extra storage cells. The normal game hotbar cannot access them after removal.

## Ammunition

Hangar ammo cartons contain 30 rifle rounds for $300. Small zombie drops contain 3 rounds; uncommon loot cartons contain 6.

Historical backpack fixes and test limitations are retained in the source's docs folder and TEST-RESULTS.txt.
