Adapted for Beyond Apocolypse: remapped and fitted skin weights and bind pose,
merged suit/head, removed the importer's broken vertex-color multiplier,
generated two simplified distance LODs, converted normal-map handedness and
baked glTF roughness/metallic factors, and omitted the source animation clips.
The game supplies its own zombie animations at runtime. No endorsement implied.
