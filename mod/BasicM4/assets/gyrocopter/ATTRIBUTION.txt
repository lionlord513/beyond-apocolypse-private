Hornet gyrocopter by Emmanuel Baranger (helijah), with original Hornet engineering data from Don Shoebridge.
FlightGear Hornet source, revision ecae170; included in the model-source companion.
GPL v2 or later; see COPYING.txt.
Modified for Beyond Apocolypse: axis/UV conversion, separate rotor/propeller, omitted baked engine, split removable blades and LOD simplification.
Original model, adaptation script and editable asset files: BeyondApocolypse-1.0.8-Hornet-Model-Source.zip, available as a separate download on the same Nexus mod page.
Players do not need to install that source download.
