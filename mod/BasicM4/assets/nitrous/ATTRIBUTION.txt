Nitrous bottle geometry was converted from the supplied
nos-nitrous-canisters.zip / clo_nitrou_cannisters.fbx model.
The supplied archive contained no author or license notice. No additional
third-party licensing claim is made here; the original code MIT license does
not grant rights to third-party bottle geometry or NOS trademarks.

Blue and purple texture atlases were enhanced with OpenAI image generation
from the supplied 256-pixel atlas. The instruction preserved UV island layout,
NOS labeling and non-painted surfaces, adding subtle enamel and surface detail.
Purple recolors the blue painted surfaces while preserving labeling.
Geometry: 504 triangles per bottle, scaled to 26 / 38 / 52 cm.
Native game material and Niagara exhaust assets are referenced at runtime,
not redistributed. The model converter is provided in tools/.
