Used as SciFi Soldier in Beyond Apocalypse. Adaptations: fitted mesh and skin
weights to the game's native body and first-person arm reference poses; isolated
first-person arms and headless body with an inner liner; smoothed exposed hands;
generated a material palette and two reduced-detail LODs; converted to glTF.
Source animations omitted; the game supplies animation. Soldier previews show
the fitted mesh posed with the native idle animation. The default portrait was
captured in Drive Beyond Horizons. No endorsement implied.

Alien character and original surface textures from Drive Beyond Horizons. The fitted first-person meshes, clothing sections and textured emissive lenses are part of this mod.
